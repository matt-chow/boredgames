# Human playtest

**Play here:** https://boredgames.mchowcl.workers.dev

**Release:** Kingdom & Commerce rules edition 4 (git tag `release-e4`). New rooms use edition 4. Rooms already in play keep the rules they started on.

## Create and join a room

1. Open the link and choose a game, then **Create room**.
2. Send the others the room link or code. Each person enters a name and takes a seat. No account is needed.
3. The host can fill empty seats with bots, choose the setup, and then **Start the game**.

If you lose connection, open the same link on the same browser and you'll return to your seat. Rules are in the **?** menu, as **Quick reference**, **How to play** and **Detailed rules**.

## Who should play

- **Kingdom & Commerce:** 2 to 6 players. Start with 3 or 4 people and the default setup. Then try 2 and 6, since those are where length and pacing differ most.
- **Bohnanza:** 3 to 5 players.
- Bots are fine for filling a seat, but people's opinions are the point of this test.

## Reporting a bug

Send the owner, or open an issue at https://github.com/matt-chow/boredgames/issues if you have access:

- The room code, the game and the number of players
- Your device and browser
- What you did, what happened, and what you expected
- A screenshot, if you have one

## What to tell us after a game

- **Length:** How long did it take? Did the end come too soon or drag on?
- **Crown Projects:** Was it clear what to fund and when? Did funding feel worthwhile, or forced? Did players get Renown they felt they'd earned?
- **Ending:** Did the final round feel fair? Did the player who triggered the ending win, and did that feel deserved?
- **Strategy:** Did more than one approach feel like it could win? Did any card or Project seem too strong, too weak or never worth buying?
- **Clarity:** Was any card, rule or screen confusing? Did you need the rules, and did you find your answer?
- **Fun:** Would you play again? What was the best moment, and the worst?

## Known limitations

- Balance comes from bot games, not people. Among bots, rushing Influence and Crown Projects is strongest, and triggering the ending usually wins (83% at 2 players, 59% at 6). Tell us if that holds with people.
- River Quay, Bell Tower and Exchange Hall are the weakest Project stages among bots.
- The Renown you can see for other players during a game is a lower bound, not their exact score.
- If someone's connection drops silently, others may wait up to about 100 seconds before a bot can step in. An idle room waits until someone returns.
- There are no saved setup presets. To repeat a market, share its market code.
- Screen-reader support is tested automatically, but no one has done a full manual pass.
- Rooms are deleted after a period with no change: 24 hours in the lobby, 30 days in play, 7 days once finished.
